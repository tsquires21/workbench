No. 08 · Odds & ends

More from the bench.

Not everything needs a whole page. These are the smaller tools, each built because a specific annoyance came up more than twice.

Built to sell

Trade calculators

Six calculator apps, for pipefitters, electricians, concrete, HVAC and more, all on one shared, tested engine. Pure math and lookup tables, with no accounts and no network. The formulas get checked by working tradespeople before anything ships.

Android

Shift calendar

A rotating-shift calendar for nurses and plant workers, built so a daylight-saving change can't knock your schedule an hour off.

Android

Row counter

A knitting and crochet counter that handles repeats inside repeats and advances them automatically. Built and running. It's waiting on a real phone to test the buzz.

Weather

Weather sentinel

A severe-weather watcher that only alerts when the warning's actual shape covers where you are, not just because your county is named.

Camper

Camper energy copilot

"Can I run this, and when does the battery die?" Answered from physics alone, with no internet needed and nothing to go out of date.

Sports

Fantasy draft engine

Ranks players by value over replacement, scored to each league's exact rules. Its keeper schedule matched the league site at every one of 210 draft slots.

Sky

Night-sky butler

Meteor showers, planets and aurora, but only when the clouds and the Moon will actually cooperate. Replaying all of 2026, it would have pinged exactly four times.

Kitchen

Recipe box

Paste a recipe link and keep just the recipe, without the life story. It reads the structured data that recipe sites publish, and it never silently converts your units.

Money

Wishlist watcher

A daily price check on things I actually want, which only speaks up on a real price drop.

Camper

Campground finder

Finds pet-friendly campgrounds that fit our trailer and truck within a couple of hours' drive, with live availability where the park allows it. Paused, because the two parks we actually use don't publish any.
